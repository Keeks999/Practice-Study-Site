📖 HOW TO USE THIS SITE

A study planner, grade tracker and flashcard trainer in one page. It takes about two minutes to set up.

🎮 Want a break? Click the ? button at the far right of the glowing top bar, then click the big glowing red ? box in that section to unlock the educational game. No code needed.

⚠ YOUR DATA STAYS ON THIS COMPUTER

Everything you add (subjects, assessments, grades, notes, flashcards) is saved only in this browser on this computer. It does not sync between computers, browsers or people, and nobody else can see it.

  • Click ⬇ Backup (top of the page) to download a backup file. Do this every week or two.
  • To use your data on another computer, or after clearing your browser, open the site there and click ⬆ Restore and pick your backup file.
  • Clearing browser data, private/incognito windows or a different browser all start empty. Your backup file is the only thing that moves your data.
  • Your Groq key is never included in a backup, so you add it again on each computer.

Quick start

1

Add your classes. Go to Assessments and click + Add subject. Type any class you take (Chemistry, AP Gov, Art, anything). Pick an icon and color.

2

Add upcoming tests and projects with + Add assessment. The closest due dates show as high priority.

3

Post your grades in the Grades section. Use any scale (/7, /10, /100). It draws trends and tells you what you need next.

4

Make flashcards and study them a little each day.

5

Make a backup with ⬇ Backup.

What each section does

📚 Assessments: your subjects and upcoming tests, quizzes and projects, sorted by how soon they are due.

💎 Grades: post scores, see averages and trends, set targets, and use the What do I need on the final? calculator.

☕ Focus Timer: study in focused blocks with breaks.

📆 Daily & 🗓️ Daily Schedule: habits you want to do every day, and your Week A / Week B class schedule.

🃏 Flashcards (Spaced Repetition): add cards, then tap a card to flip it and rate how well you knew it (Again, Hard, Good, Easy). Cards you know return after longer and longer gaps. Cards you miss return soon. Keys: Space flips, 1 to 4 rate.

📝 Notes: write and search class notes. They save as you type.

📅 Calendar: a calendar of everything that is due.

🧭 AI Study Assistant: the study-sheet analyzer and the study chat now live together. Paste a sheet on the left and the AI tells you what to study first and what to do next (it can also turn the sheet into flashcards). Ask the tutor about any topic on the right.

🎯 Quiz Arena: quick practice quizzes.

🎮 Educational Game: click the round ? button at the far right of the top bar, then click the big glowing red ? box in that section (no code needed). It unlocks the game section, where you can download and play Sovereign Roll, a strategy board game of diplomacy and resources.

🧭 Getting around: the whole site is one long page. Use the buttons on the glowing top bar to jump to any section, the ▲ ▼ arrows on the right edge to move one section at a time, and the 🌙 Dark / ☀ Light switch to change the look. Scroll down fast and a Jump to top button appears.

🔔 Reminders: click Enable notifications (top of the page) and your computer pops up a reminder 1 day before each assessment. The bar goes away once you have clicked it.

🔑 Getting a free Groq key (for the AI Study Assistant)

  1. Go to console.groq.com and sign in.
  2. Open API Keys and click Create API Key. Copy it (it starts with gsk_).
  3. Here, click 🔑 Add Groq key, paste it, and press Save.

The key stays in this browser and is only sent to Groq. Treat it like a password and do not share it. The AI is for school topics only: unsafe or inappropriate requests are blocked immediately.
